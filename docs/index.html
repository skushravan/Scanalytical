Economic Dynamics // Price Elasticity

Super-Fan Inelasticity at Premium Price Points

While budget shows (<$75) average 14,000 attendees, VIP tiers ($250+) generate the highest gross yield without sacrificing fill rates.

Model Evaluation // Fill Rate Prediction (Un-Biased)

Eliminating Capacity Target Leakage Reveals True Drivers

When models predict raw attendance while including venue capacity as an input, capacity artificially consumes >98% of feature splits due to target leakage (attendance ≈ capacity). By training our 150-tree Random Forest to predict Venue Fill Rate (%) without capacity in the feature vector, the model reveals the true hierarchy of demand: Venue Scale (27.8%), Artist Fame Tier (21.0%), Genre (13.1%), Calendar Timing (11.4%), Ticket Price (9.7%), and Market Depth (9.6%).

// DATA PROVENANCE & METHODOLOGY

Rigorous Data Pipeline & Modeling Architecture

1. Data Acquisition & Kaggle Provenance

This project ingests and unifies primary open datasets sourced from Kaggle: gayu14/taylor-concert-tours-impact-on-attendance-and (historical world tours), tymonbot/taylor-swift-eras-toure (Eras Tour official ticket sales & coordinates), jessalynlim/ed-sheeran-tour (Mathematics Tour boxscores), and rodolfobrandao95/oasis-live-25 (Oasis Live '25 world tour financials). These datasets were enriched with official Billboard Boxscore and Pollstar reporting tables for Coldplay, Beyoncé, Harry Styles, The Weeknd, U2, and Elton John to ensure global diversity across musical genres and venue structures.

2. Data Harmonization & Rescaling

Multi-night residency boxscore aggregations (e.g. 10 shows reported as a single lump-sum boxscore) were algorithmically decomposed into single-show attendance, physical capacity per night, gross per show, and average ticket price. Geocoding was matched against a curated 320-city database assigning standard sovereign countries, continents, latitude/longitude, and market tiers.

3. Predictive Machine Learning (Un-Biased Fill Rate Modeling)

To prevent target leakage where venue capacity artificially dwarfs all behavioral metrics (since attendance ≈ capacity × fill rate), our 150-tree Random Forest regressor is trained directly on Venue Fill Rate (%) without capacity in the feature vector. Evaluation on an 80/20 train/test split yielded an R² of 0.7523 and an MAE of ±4.55%, uncovering the true causal hierarchy: Venue Scale (27.8%), Artist Star Power (21.0%), Genre Demographics (13.1%), Calendar Timing (11.4%), and Pricing Elasticity (9.7%).
